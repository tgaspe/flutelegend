Delete your FluteLegend account

You can delete your FluteLegend account and the data linked to it at any time, from the app or by email.

Deletion is permanent. Your scores, statistics, learning path and Memory Mode progress are erased and cannot be restored.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open FluteLegend on your Android device or iPhone.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

Your account and its data are deleted straight away. If you signed in with Apple, you confirm with Apple first, and FluteLegend's Sign in with Apple access is revoked.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email support@chewpacastudios.com with the subject Account Deletion Request. Include your FluteLegend username, and the Google Play Games name or the Apple Account email address you signed in with.

We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email when it's done.
