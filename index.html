How it works

  1. 1See the fingering

    Notes scroll toward you, and a chart under the highway shows which holes to cover for the next one.

  2. 2Play the note

    The microphone hears what you play and shows the note you hit, so you know when you’re sharp, flat or an octave off.

  3. 3Learn song by song

    A path of short levels brings in new notes one at a time, with well-known songs, slower practice speeds and a step-by-step mode.
